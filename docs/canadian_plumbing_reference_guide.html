Canadian Plumbing Reference Guide

This guide provides a concise overview of common plumbing concepts, materials and practices used across Canada. It is intended as a quick reference and does not replace local building codes or professional advice.

National and Provincial Codes

Plumbing installations in Canada must comply with the National Plumbing Code of Canada (NPC) as adopted and modified by each province or territory. Always consult the local code authority for regional requirements.

  • Refer to provincial amendments for variations from the NPC.
  • Some municipalities publish additional guidelines or checklists.

Fixture Rough-In Dimensions (Metric)

Measurements below are in millimetres and reflect common practice. Verify against local requirements.

  • Water closet flange: 305 mm from finished rear wall to flange centre.
  • Lavatory drain: Rough-in at 500–600 mm above finished floor.
  • Kitchen sink drain: Approximately 450 mm above finished floor.
  • Shower drain: Centre placement within the shower base, typical 2–4 % slope to drain.

Installation Tips

  1. Always flush new water lines to remove debris before connecting fixtures.
  2. Support horizontal drains every 1.2 m to maintain proper slope.
  3. Use Teflon tape or pipe dope on threaded connections.
  4. Protect pipes from freezing in unheated spaces by insulating or providing heat trace.

Resources

Useful references include provincial building code publications, municipal permit offices, and professional trade associations such as the Canadian Institute of Plumbing and Heating (CIPH).
